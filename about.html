Our Story

How a Family Recipe Became a Beloved Shop.

It started in a small kitchen, with a recipe passed down from a grandmother in Madrid — a simple mix of flour, water and salt that somehow turned into the most memorable churro any of us had ever tasted.

In 2016, we opened our first little shop on Sugar Lane. A handful of tables, a counter, and a fryer that never got a break. Word spread fast — first from neighbours, then from all over the city.

Today we've grown, but the recipe hasn't changed. Every churro still starts with that same simple batter, fried the same way it was in that tiny Madrid kitchen — because some things are simply too good to change.

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Vision.

The two things that guide every churro we make and every choice we take as a team.

Our Mission

To turn everyday moments into something a little sweeter — one fresh, golden, made-with-care churro at a time. We exist to make people smile from the very first bite, and we take that seriously.

Our Vision

To be the churro shop that cities talk about — known not just for incredible dessert, but for the warmth of the people behind the counter and the feeling you get the moment you walk through the door.
